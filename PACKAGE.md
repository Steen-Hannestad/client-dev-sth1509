# client-dev-sth1509 — what this package is

A curated snapshot of the `client_dev` working tree as of 15 September 2026, prepared for
upstream. It carries the validated changes and deliberately omits three experimental lines
that were investigated but not shown to work.

## Included

**Iterative-training changes** (documented in `CHANGES.md`)

* **Dynamic `c`** — the ESS-floored `msre` loss, `training.msre_ess_floor`. Default on.
* **Peak injection at iteration 0** — `prior.n_inject`.
* **Continuous re-injection** — `acquisition.n_inject`, re-fitting the quadratic on the
  whole accumulated training set every iteration.
* **Learning-rate annealing** — `training.lr_schedule`, default on. 2.1× lower validation
  loss *and* 2.4× better credible metric at a matched epoch budget; the only change tested
  here where a training gain reached the metric. Includes a schedule-gated stopper, because
  a plain `EarlyStopping` cannot terminate an annealed run.

**Speed and memory changes** (documented in `PERFORMANCE.md`, with measurements)

* Preallocated sampler chain buffer — high-water mark from 2.93× one chain to 1×;
  29D peak resident from ~6.8 GB to a measured 3.8–4.4 GB.
* `sampling.thin` — strides at write time so a thinned chain never allocates the
  unthinned one. **Newly reachable**: the sampler supported it but no config field existed.
* `acquisition.batch_size` — batched density-deficit selection. On a 32 100-point request
  the selection went from 3 483 s to 277 s (12.6×) with per-parameter KS against the exact
  selection below 0.01 and no measurable intra-set clustering.
* BLAS-identity distances in `select_points`, squared throughout, one `argmax` instead of
  two, incremental re-maximisation — 3.97× end to end, exact (8.8e-16 at the r_k scale).
* Streaming `summarize()` in the convergence metric, run-length deduplication in
  acquisition, no full-array MPI broadcast, explicit per-iteration lifetime management.
* Defaults `n_walkers: 432, thin: 5` in the shipped research configs: measured 2.04× the
  effective sample size at unchanged τ (3610 vs 3678) and no wall-time penalty, in 40% of
  the memory.

**All likelihood targets investigated in the project** — `input/cobaya/`: the analytic
banana family (`banana_function.py`: 29D, 31D with the parabolic ridge, and the 12D/16D
reductions) plus the inline Gaussians (27D Planck, 25D conditioned variants, 56D and 58D
Euclid). The analytic targets need no Boltzmann solver.

## Deliberately excluded

* **Input whitening** (`model.whiten`, `whiten_partial`, the `WhitenInputs` layer and its
  covariance builders). A large training win — 18–45× validation loss — that made the
  credible metric **worse**: on the 31D ridge max ΔCM went 0.2994 → 0.7760 (full) and
  1.3948 (partial). Whitening reparameterises the target into radial form, which is exact
  only where one covariance describes the posterior everywhere.
* **Model transfer** (M1/M2/M3, `sampling/transfer_design.py`, `dataset/transfer_data.py`
  and the `transfer:` config block). Only M2 was ever scored, and its benefit proved
  non-monotonic in iteration. The four transfer-study configs were removed with it, because
  a retained `transfer:` block would now be **silently ignored** and run as a plain arm.
* **Alternative optimisers** — Muon and an Adam variant carrying a K-FAC style input
  preconditioner. Muon measured **10.6× slower per epoch**; the preconditioner recovered
  only 11–55% of the whitening effect that was itself harmful. `build_optimizer` is Adam.

Note that the *acquisition* whitening — `_WhitenedKNN`, the metric the density-deficit
selector estimates distances in — and the whitened coordinates of the quadratic peak fit
are **core machinery and are retained**. They share a word with the excluded feature and
nothing else.

Signatures also narrowed, which is a breaking change for anyone calling them directly:

* `build_model(inputs, targets, n_layers, n_neurons, activation)` — lost `base_model`,
  `base_index`, `resid_floor`, `whiten`, `whiten_stats`, `whiten_exclude`, and no longer
  builds a residual head.
* `build_optimizer(learning_rate)` — lost `name`, `muon_ns_steps`, `precond`, `precond_key`.
  Always Adam.
* `train_model(...)` — lost `optimizer`, `muon_ns_steps`.
* `build_initial_design(cfg, likelihood, master)` — lost the transfer context argument; it
  is now the Latin hypercube path only.

### Config keys removed

`model.whiten`, `model.whiten_partial`, `model.whiten_n_exclude`, `training.optimizer`,
`training.muon_ns_steps`, and the whole top-level `transfer:` block. `ModelConfig` is down
to `n_layers`, `n_neurons`, `activation`.

### Retained despite sharing a name

`_WhitenedKNN` in `dataset/dataset.py` and its use throughout `dataset/acquisition.py` is
the metric the density-deficit selector estimates distances in; `dataset/peak_samples.py`
fits its quadratic in whitened coordinates; `convergence/convergence.py` whitens the drift
delta by the covariance. **All three are core machinery** — the second is what peak
injection depends on. They share a word with the excluded feature and nothing else, so a
grep-and-delete over "whiten" would break the acquisition.

### Not carried over

`tests/test_acquisition_equivalence.py` and its frozen baseline, which compare against the
pre-optimisation implementation and only make sense inside the development history. A
regression test for the selector should be rebuilt against this package's own behaviour.

`environment.yaml` was **rewritten** rather than copied: the `client_dev` version pinned
tensorflow 2.20.0 and pandas 2.2.3, neither of which ran, and omitted cobaya, emcee and
h5py entirely.

## Verified

* All 29 modules import; all 32 run configs parse.
* `python tests/test_lr_schedule.py` — five tests covering the annealing callbacks.
* `python client.py input/_smoke.yaml -n smoke -i 2` runs the full loop end to end on the
  16D analytic banana in about 20 s, exercising the LHC design, iteration-0 injection,
  dynamic `c`, training, thinned sampling, the drift metric, batched selection and
  continuous re-injection.
* `environment.yaml` was rewritten: the version in the source tree pinned tensorflow 2.20.0
  and pandas 2.2.3 — neither of which ran — and omitted cobaya, emcee and h5py, so an
  environment built from it could not evaluate a likelihood or write a chain.
